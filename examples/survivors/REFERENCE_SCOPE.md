# Reference scope: Vampire Survivors richness

This note uses poncle’s Steam announcements as the source of record. It separates the October 2022 v1.0 launch baseline from the current stable base game and does not count paid DLC content as base-game breadth. It is a mechanic benchmark, not a claim that matching catalog totals would establish equivalent richness.

## Two different reference points

| Reference point | Verified boundary | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **v1.0 launch, 20 Oct 2022** | poncle described v1.0 as completion of the game’s main content. The launch patch added one event stage and one weapon; its notes refer to five main stages. These are patch additions and stage categories, not total catalog counts. | The launch game already had standard weapon evolutions **and unions**, plus two global run variants, Inverse and Endless. The patch notes do not publish total weapons, characters, passives, Arcanas, stages, enemies, bosses, or achievements. Do not infer those totals from the “one new weapon” line. ([v1.0 release note](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4588629064110933223), [v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841)) |
| **Current stable base game, as of 22 Oct 2026** | The latest stable version announced on 16 Oct 2026 is 1.16.111; the same announcement describes 1.17 content as public beta, so it is excluded here. Free updates since launch add more base-game systems. | This is a much larger target than the original 1.0 snapshot. Paid DLC additions remain outside the comparison. ([current stable update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/1846651706681529), [Steam store page and add-ons](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/)) |

The official v1.0 notes make the run modifiers concrete. Inverse turns the stage layout upside down, raises gold and Luck, starts enemies at much higher health, and scales them further over time; it also changes merchant stock. Endless removes the final Reaper encounter, loops the wave schedule, increases enemy numbers, health and damage each cycle, and brings the merchant back with a different offer. These are independent run rules, not five different backdrops. The same notes connect Arcanas to weapons and stats: for example, they call out Bracelet evolutions reacting to specific Arcanas and Arcana explosion damage scaling with Curse. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))

## What current base-game updates add

The v1.14 announcement lists two themed stages (Westwoods and Mazerella), two characters, two weapons with evolutions, a character morph, a power-up, three Darkanas, Party Mode, and the Survarot modifier mechanic. The announcement explicitly describes Survarots as another character-themed bonus system, similar in broad role to Arcana. Party Mode is part of the reference game’s breadth but is outside this project’s single-player requirement. ([poncle’s v1.14 / Ante Chamber announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/1814309641613560))

The free v1.15 update adds The Lycaeum, two characters, three Darkanas, and three relics. Its weapon count is written jokingly as “either 2 or 10,” so this note treats it as unverified. More useful as a richness example is Para Kooleo’s special run: a six-form tuna weapon, evolutions that transform Vacuum pickups, and a mode in which defeated enemies yield spendable coins that also serve as health while experience gems stop leveling the character. This changes the run’s economy and progression loop, not just damage values. ([poncle’s v1.15 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/693137145499484494))

These examples show why “current base” and “v1.0 launch” must stay separate. They do **not** justify including paid additions such as Legacy of the Moonspell or Legacy of the Bloodmoon in the comparison; Steam lists them as separate add-ons. The current update log also announces those paid expansions separately. ([Steam add-on list](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/), [Legacy of the Bloodmoon announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/1842212951303118))

## Where the current own-content plan is thin

The plan of 24 weapons and 24 evolutions, 18 passives, 12 Arcanas, 8 heroes, 5 arenas, 20 enemies, 10 bosses, 10 meta upgrades, and 20 achievements is a useful catalog target. It does not yet name several structural sources of variety present in the reference:

1. **Weapon unions are a separate build path from single-weapon evolution.** The 1.0 game tracked discovery of both standard evolutions and unions. The current plan names evolutions but no unions. Add a set of recipes that fuse two developed weapons into a new outcome; make the result change attack behavior or the build’s role, not only its numbers. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))
2. **Arena-specific conditions do not replace global run modifiers.** The plan has five arenas with their own conditions but no reusable run-wide variants. Add at least two modifiers with distinct rule effects, such as one that changes layout, starting enemy pressure and merchant options, and another that loops the wave schedule while escalating danger and changing the run endpoint. Make them compose with every arena. This is the clearest launch-baseline gap. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))
3. **Build systems need cross-links.** The plan has many passive items and Arcanas, but its requirements do not define a synergy network. Give every evolution a recipe and ensure some Arcanas alter specific weapon behaviors, status effects, pickups or enemy interactions; test recipes and effects as a matrix rather than only checking that each catalog entry exists. The launch notes’ Arcana, weapon and Curse interactions are direct examples. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))
4. **Stage breadth needs run structure.** Five visually distinct arenas are not equivalent to five distinct run experiences. Give each arena a unique wave schedule, at least one interactable objective or stage pickup, and a boss or elite encounter whose behavior fits that arena. Include at least one separate event or challenge configuration; v1.0 distinguishes five main stages from its event stage. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))
5. **Hero count needs strategic identity.** Eight heroes are enough as a catalog count only if their starting attacks or traits encourage different early choices and evolution targets. The reference’s current content demonstrates that a character can alter the whole progression economy, as Para Kooleo does. A practical review gate is that every hero has one behavior that changes a run decision, not only a small stat bonus. ([v1.15 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/693137145499484494))
6. **Enemy and boss counts need encounter roles.** Twenty enemy names and ten boss names do not guarantee variety if most differ only by health and speed. Ensure the set covers distinct pressure patterns (pursuit, ranged zoning, crowding, durable blockers, timed elites, and boss phases or arena hazards) and that each map schedules those patterns differently.
7. **Unlocks should form a discovery path.** Meta upgrades and achievements should lead through stage relics, characters, weapons, run variants and secrets. The v1.0 event-stage path itself uses repeated stage visits to unlock and try its new modes; it is more than an achievement counter. Keep the project’s 20-achievement target as a catalog minimum, but judge progression by whether new content is reached through varied, visible goals. ([v1.0 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1794680/view/4714731755412623841))

## Operational acceptance floor

For the single-player clone, a defensible minimum richness gate is:

- The standard loop supports weapon-plus-passive evolutions **and** multi-weapon unions, with materially different results.
- At least two global run modifiers change the danger curve, encounter schedule, economy, layout, or ending, and can be combined with the arenas.
- Each arena has its own wave plan, objective or pickup, and encounter identity; one event/challenge run reuses content under different rules.
- Hero traits, passive items and Arcana effects create observable build choices across the run, including weapon-specific interactions.
- Persistent unlocks expose new heroes, weapons, arenas and modifiers through several kinds of goals, not just one repeated survival threshold.
- Catalog review checks behavior coverage and interactions, not just the planned entry counts.

The public sources cited here do not establish exact total catalog counts for v1.0 or the current base game. Any later numerical comparison should be added only if poncle’s own notes or current official game data directly support it.
