## Evidence and design inference

### Jimin

**Verified.** In his first-person Weverse interview, Jimin describes wanting the emotion in his dance, actions and singing to reach the audience, and discusses shaping performance around that expression. This supports emotional precision as a theme; it does not support a literal defensive power. [Weverse Magazine, Jimin interview](https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/56?lang=en).

**Verified.** BIGHIT describes MUSE as a search for inspiration following the identity exploration of FACE. Its official presentation supplies a broader expressive theme, rather than a mandate to copy an album logo or title. [BIGHIT release notice](https://weverse.io/bts/notice/20329), [official MUSE page](https://ibighit.com/legacy/jimin/muse-ko.html).

**Verified reference material.** BANGTANTV's official 2018 MMA episode is the primary performance reference to inspect for the fan motif. The fan is proposed as an adaptation of stage imagery, not a reproduction of an official merchandise item. [BANGTANTV, 2018 MMA episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hi7o-cnmBZU).

**Verified.** The official BT21 page describes CHIMMY's yellow hoodie and persistent effort. Those are useful supporting temperament cues. The proposed icon deliberately avoids CHIMMY's face, hoodie silhouette and name. [BT21 character descriptions](https://www.bt21.com/character).

**Design inference.** A fan combines grace with a hard directional edge and also explains the existing outgoing/returning attack. Swanward suggests a graceful guardian, but the swan association is less specific than the physical fan. Keep it as a candidate until a blind identification test; do not assume that a cleaner invented name is automatically recognizable.

### V

**Verified.** V directly describes jazz as free-form and unplanned, and says that he chooses photos and posts according to how he feels that day. He also explains a desire to convey freedom through an imagined movie of his life. [Weverse Magazine, V interview](https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/436?lang=en).

**Verified.** The label's Canadian distributor describes the Layover focus track as 1970s romantic soul with a relaxed, free-spirited character. This is a first-party release description, not a fan theory. [Universal Music Canada release](https://www.universalmusic.ca/2023/08/08/v-of-bts-announces-debut-solo-album-layover-out-september-8/). The official BIGHIT page is a visual reference; its image-heavy page did not expose useful descriptive copy in the browser reader. [Official Layover page](https://ibighit.com/legacy/v/layover-en.html).

**Verified.** TATA is described as curious, supernatural and highly elastic on the official BT21 page. That supports the user's surreal contrast, but does not establish that V himself should have the same literal power. [BT21 character descriptions](https://www.bt21.com/character).

**Design inference.** A viewfinder is more precise than another generic magic gem. It ties photography to a visible spatial frame while leaving room for an impossible second exposure. Keep V's brass/mauve framing distinct from RM's blue page shapes and Jin's moon-and-star silhouette.

### Jung Kook

**Verified.** Jung Kook describes accepting mistakes and continuing to practice rather than remaining trapped by them. That supports a repeatable improvement loop rather than a passive claim that he is already perfect. [Weverse Magazine, solo-single interview](https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/825?lang=en).

**Verified.** His own account connects GOLDEN with inherited talent, a dream his mother had, and a particularly bright stage of his life. Gold is therefore a sourced visual cue; the proposed nickname does not reuse the complete album title. [Vogue Japan first-person interview](https://www.vogue.co.jp/article/2023-december-bts-jung-kook-interview-english).

**Verified.** Boxing is explicitly discussed as stamina training in his interview and appears in an official BANGTANTV clip. A glove is consequently a concrete personal reference rather than a generic weapon selected only because the game needs damage. [Weverse Magazine, Jung Kook interview](https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/433?lang=en), [BANGTANTV boxing clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ag_mS0Ygzg).

**Verified.** COOKY's official description emphasizes stamina, heart and dependability. Use this as supporting context for the playful athletic contrast; do not copy its character face into the icon. [BT21 character descriptions](https://www.bt21.com/character).

**Design inference.** Gold plus a hare clue should be easier for fans than an opaque fantasy coinage, but it still needs a blind test. A glove also gives the clearest silhouette distinction from Jimin's wide fan and V's hollow rectangle.

## Naming screen

Exact quoted web searches were run on 2026-10-26. The screen records visible naming collisions, not registered-mark status. Search engines sometimes return approximate matches even for quoted queries. “No clear exact product surfaced” means only that the returned results did not provide one. It does not mean the name is unused, unregistered, or available in any jurisdiction. Trademark database searches, sound-alike checks and relevant product classes were not completed. ## Icon construction and review criteria

Use one shared 32-unit viewbox and dark outline family. Give trinkets one physical object silhouette. Ability icons add one readable indication of what the object does; do not reuse the exact trinket image for every ability.

| Character | Trinket | Attack icon | Innate icon | Minimum-size checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jimin | Open five-panel fan with central hinge. | The same fan with a single returning curved trail. No tiny arrowheads at 16 px. | A folded fan held in front of a small solid guard plate. | At 16 px retain the scalloped upper edge and handle; at 24 px retain at least three panel divisions; at 32 px show five panels and one rim highlight. |
| V | Brass viewfinder with open center and side grip. | A hollow frame splitting into three outward rays. | A skewed small clock nested beside the frame, with a clear clock hand. | The central aperture must stay open at 16 px; the silhouette must not become a plus or a crosshair. At 24/32 px preserve the asymmetric side grip. |
| Jung Kook | Gold boxing glove with distinct cuff. | Forward-facing glove and one broad trailing streak. | A glove cuff encircling a simple heart shape. | At 16 px keep thumb notch and cuff division; at 24 px preserve the pink seam; at 32 px use a restrained metallic highlight. Do not depend on rabbit ears to read “glove.” |

First review the icons without labels in monochrome, then in their character palettes, then on the actual menu/HUD backgrounds at 16, 24 and 32 px. Ask an independent image reviewer what each object is and what action it suggests before revealing the intended character. Record object recognition, mechanical meaning and fan association as separate judgments. A fan that reads clearly is not automatically recognizable as Jimin.

## Proposed mechanical changes

These are optional prototype specifications. The existing kits should remain available as a baseline for comparison.

### Jimin: Reprise

Keep the current fan trajectory. The outgoing fan places one short-lived mark on each enemy it hits. The returning fan consumes marks made by its own cast for 30% additional return-hit damage. Marks expire when that fan ends, preventing permanent stacking. No invulnerability, parry window or manual timing input is promised.

The intended feeling is careful looping and turning pursuers into a return path. A visible fan-rib glint on marked enemies is preferable to another status symbol over every head. In co-op, mark ownership must include player and cast identity. Prototype first with three stationary target clusters so outgoing/returning hit semantics can be observed.

### V: Double Exposure

A radial cast creates a non-colliding frame at its origin. After 0.45 seconds, that fixed frame emits a second burst rotated by half the original angular spacing. The second burst deals 50% of the original damage. Limit each cast to one delayed echo and prevent echoes from creating further echoes.

The intended feeling is leaving a strange composition behind while moving away. This uses explicit delayed emissions and stored cast positions, not random teleportation. Enemies should see a warm frame warning before the echo; the object must not resemble a safe zone or portal that the player can enter. In co-op, the delayed frame retains the caster's ownership for damage and rewards.

### Jung Kook: Clean Combo

Three successful starter hits within two seconds trigger one longer, piercing straight punch along the most recent successful shot direction. Taking damage or letting the timer expire resets the streak. Each projectile can contribute at most one hit, preventing a single multi-target projectile from instantly completing the combo. The finisher cannot add to its own counter.

The intended feeling is steady execution that grows into a clean finish. Start with three visible cuff stitches filling in rather than a second large meter. The new tooltip would be “Land three starter hits quickly to fire a piercing punch.” Keep the current passive bonuses until balance testing establishes whether the finisher needs compensation.
